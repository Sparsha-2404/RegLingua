# Verification policy (Verifier agent contract)

Source of truth: `VERIFICATION_POLICY` sheet. Verdicts are PASS / FAIL / REVIEW; REVIEW is preferred over guessing (VP11). Retries are bounded at 2, then the case is marked NEEDS_REVIEW.

| POLICY_ID | CHECK | PASS CONDITION | FAIL CONDITION | REVIEW CONDITION | VERIFIER ACTION |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| VP01 | Evidence presence | At least one selected evidence chunk is present and has SOURCE_ID, SECTION and SOURCE_URL. | No evidence is returned, or evidence metadata is missing. | Evidence exists but relevance to the claim is uncertain. | FAIL when absent; REVIEW when relevance cannot be established. |
| VP02 | Evidence support | The cited source passage directly supports the regulatory claim made in the explanation. | The explanation states a requirement not supported by the cited passage. | Support is partial, conditional, or depends on facts not present in the case. | PASS only for direct support; otherwise FAIL or REVIEW. |
| VP03 | Source traceability | SOURCE_ID, document title/section and URL resolve to a catalogued regulatory source. | Citation cannot be traced to the regulatory source catalog. | Source exists but version/effective-date applicability is uncertain. | FAIL if untraceable; REVIEW if version applicability is uncertain. |
| VP04 | Risk consistency | Final risk label is consistent with the cited requirement and case facts. | Risk label contradicts the evidence and facts. | Risk depends on missing facts or conditions. | PASS, FAIL, or REVIEW respectively; do not force ambiguous cases. |
| VP05 | Rewrite addresses issue | Rewrite removes or corrects the specific risky behavior identified in the original statement. | The risky behavior remains materially unchanged. | The rewrite changes the scenario so much that improvement cannot be established. | FAIL if issue remains; REVIEW if comparison is inconclusive. |
| VP06 | No unsupported additions | Rewrite does not introduce a new legal/regulatory claim unsupported by evidence. | Rewrite adds a regulatory obligation not supported by the selected evidence. | New wording may be accurate but requires another source to establish it. | FAIL for unsupported new claims; REVIEW if another citation is needed. |
| VP07 | Before/after risk | Compliant rewrite is expected to reduce the defined risk signal from HIGH to LOW, or MEDIUM to LOW where appropriate. | Risk remains HIGH or becomes higher without justification. | Risk remains MEDIUM because material context is unresolved. | FAIL for unjustified HIGH; REVIEW for unresolved MEDIUM. |
| VP08 | Language preservation | Meaning and regulatory intent are preserved across English, Hindi and hi-en variants. | Translation changes substantive compliance meaning. | Translation is ambiguous or linguistically unclear. | FAIL for meaning drift; REVIEW for ambiguity. |
| VP09 | No hallucinated quotation | Paraphrases are presented as paraphrases unless they exactly match a source quotation. | Generated text is presented as a quotation without matching source text. | Wording is too close to source to determine quotation vs paraphrase. | FAIL if falsely quoted; REVIEW if wording needs clarification. |
| VP10 | Freshness/version check | The cited source is the applicable/current version for the evaluation case. | A repealed/outdated source is used when a current source is required. | Historical source is used and current applicability is unclear. | FAIL for wrong current version; REVIEW where historical applicability matters. |
| VP11 | Abstention rule | Verifier can establish evidence support, risk consistency and rewrite improvement. | Verifier establishes a material contradiction or unsupported claim. | Verifier lacks enough evidence/context to decide confidently. | PASS, FAIL, or REVIEW; REVIEW is preferred over guessing. |
| VP12 | Finalization gate | All required checks pass and no unresolved REVIEW condition remains. | Any mandatory check fails. | One or more checks remain unresolved. | FINALIZE only on PASS; otherwise route to REWRITE or NEEDS_REVIEW. |
