# RegLingua - Risk Annotation Policy (M1)

## Low

**Definition:** Generally compliant or low-concern statement that aligns with the cited regulatory requirement.

**Annotation rule:** Label Low when the statement describes an allowed practice or a practice that satisfies the relevant regulatory requirement.

**Example:** We recommend investments that match your risk profile.

**Notes:** Low does not mean officially approved by the regulator; it is a project-level label.

## Medium

**Definition:** Statement may require additional context, disclosure, assessment, or conditions before it can be considered compliant.

**Annotation rule:** Label Medium when compliance depends on missing information, context, or additional safeguards.

**Example:** Our experts provide investment advice based on your financial goals.

**Notes:** Medium indicates that further review or context may be needed.

## High

**Definition:** Statement describes a practice that conflicts with, bypasses, or creates significant compliance concern under the cited regulatory requirement.

**Annotation rule:** Label High when the statement indicates a clear failure to follow an applicable requirement or a materially risky practice.

**Example:** Our platform recommends investments without asking about your financial situation.

**Notes:** High is a project annotation label, not an official regulator-issued risk category.
